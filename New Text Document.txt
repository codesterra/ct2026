5 Useful Free Online Tools and Resources for Developers, Designers, and Gamers

Finding the right online tool can save time, simplify everyday tasks, and improve productivity. Here are five useful websites for different needs, from image editing and color selection to coding, calculations, and Palworld breeding.

1. BestColorPicker — https://bestcolorpicker.online/

A useful resource for designers and developers who work with colors, color palettes, and website design. Explore color-related tools to help choose and work with suitable colors for digital projects.

2. ImageFix — https://imagefix.site/

An online image tools platform for common tasks such as image compression, resizing, cropping, format conversion, and image optimization. Useful for bloggers, website owners, designers, and social media creators.

3. ObfuscatorTools — https://obfuscatortools.com/

A collection of online code obfuscation and deobfuscation tools for developers working with different programming languages. Useful for exploring code transformation and readability-related workflows.

4. PalPedia — https://palpedia.top/

A resource for Palworld players looking for Pal information, breeding combinations, and breeding-related tools. It can help players explore combinations and plan their in-game breeding strategies.

5. TopCalculators — https://topcalculators.top/

An online calculator resource for solving practical calculation problems. Useful for students, homeowners, DIY enthusiasts, and anyone who needs quick calculations for everyday projects.
